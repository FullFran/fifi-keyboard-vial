# Product Guidelines - Fifi Keyboard Project

## 1. Tone & Voice
*   **Encouraging & Welcoming:** Documentation and landing page copy should feel accessible to newcomers. Use supportive language that lowers the barrier to entry for handwiring projects.
*   **Minimalist & Focused:** Avoid clutter. Information should be presented in a clean, direct manner, ensuring that the most important details (build steps, keymap visualizer) are front and center.
*   **Technical but Clear:** While the project involves hardware and firmware, avoid jargon where a simpler explanation suffices. When technical terms are necessary, provide context.

## 2. Visual Identity (Landing Page)
*   **Minimalist Aesthetic:** Use a clean layout with plenty of white space. Focus on high-quality photography of the keyboard or crisp vector representations.
*   **Intuitive Interactions:** The keymap visualizer should be the hero of the page—reactive, fast, and easy to understand without a manual.
*   **Consistent Styling:** Adhere to a cohesive color palette and typography that reflects a modern, "custom keyboard" feel.

## 3. Documentation Standards
*   **Step-by-Step Guidance:** Build guides should be broken down into manageable phases (e.g., Matrix Wiring, Controller Preparation, Firmware Flashing).
*   **Visual-First:** Use diagrams or photos liberally to explain the handwiring process.
*   **Troubleshooting Support:** Proactively address common issues (e.g., orientation of diodes, shorts in the matrix) with a helpful tone.

## 4. Code & Firmware Quality
*   **Clarity over Complexity:** Maintain the Vial-QMK configuration such that it is easy to read and modify by others.
*   **Self-Documenting Code:** Use clear variable names and concise comments in `config.h` and `keymap.c` to explain non-standard settings.
